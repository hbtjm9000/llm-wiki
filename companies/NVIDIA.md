## Blackwell Platform Economics (vs Hopper)
| Metric | Hopper (HGX H200) | Blackwell (GB300 NVL72) | Improvement |
|--------|------------------|------------------------|-------------|
| Cost per GPU/hour | $1.41 | $2.65 | 2x |
| FLOPS per dollar | 2.8 PFLOPS | 5.6 PFLOPS | 2x |
| Tokens/sec/GPU | 90 | 6,000 | **65x** |
| Tokens/sec/MW | 54K | 2.8M | **50x** |
| Cost per million tokens | $4.20 | $0.12 | **35x lower** |
