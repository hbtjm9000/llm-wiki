# Waymo

**Type:** Company  
**Industry:** Autonomous Vehicles / Self-Driving Technology  
**Founded:** 2009 (as Google Self-Driving Car Project)  
**Headquarters:** Mountain View, CA  
**Parent:** Alphabet Inc.  
**Status:** Subsidiary  

## Overview

Waymo is a leading autonomous driving technology company developing self-driving systems for ride-hailing, trucking, and personal vehicles. One of the most advanced and commercially deployed autonomous vehicle companies.

## Recent Activity

- **Q1 2026**: Raised significant funding as part of the $300B venture quarter (one of four mega-deals totaling $188B)
- Part of the 80% of venture capital flowing to AI companies in Q1 2026
- Represents the physical layer of AI (vs. pure software) - requires fleets and infrastructure

## Key Products
