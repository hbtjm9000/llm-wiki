# Ensemble

**Type:** Company  
**Industry:** Enterprise AI / Healthcare Technology  
**Founded:** Unknown  
**Headquarters:** Unknown  
**Status:** Private  

## Overview

Ensemble is an enterprise AI company focused on embedding intelligence into operational platforms, particularly in healthcare and services domains. Advocates for treating AI as an operating layer rather than a utility.

## Recent Activity

- **April 2026**: Published thought leadership on enterprise AI operating layer approach via MIT Technology Review
- Promotes knowledge distillation methodology for converting expert judgment into training signals

## Key Products

- AI operating layer platforms
- Knowledge distillation systems
- Healthcare AI solutions (implied)

## Philosophy

**Core Thesis:** The durable advantage in enterprise AI is structural - who owns the operating layer where intelligence is applied, governed, and improved - not which foundation model is used.

**Approach:**
- Embed AI directly into operational platforms
- Instrument platforms so work generates usable signals
- Convert expert judgment into machine-readable training signals
- Build feedback loops where every exception/correction becomes learning

## Competitive Landscape

**Positioning:** Contrasts with model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) that sell intelligence as stateless API service

**Advantage:** Incumbents with operational data and domain expertise can build defensible AI systems
