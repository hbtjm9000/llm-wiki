# Enterprise AI as Operating Layer

## Source

MIT Technology Review: [Treating enterprise AI as an operating layer](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/04/16/1135554/treating-enterprise-ai-as-an-operating-layer/)

## Core Thesis

Two models:
1. **AI as utility** (OpenAI, Anthropic) - on-demand, stateless, interchangeable
2. **AI as operating layer** - compounds with use, embeds into operations

## Key Inversion

**Traditional:** Humans use software → judgment is the product
**AI-native:** AI executes → humans adjudicate edge cases

## Three Compounding Assets (Incumbent Moats)

1. **Proprietary operational data**
2. **Domain experts** generating training signals
3. **Tacit knowledge** about how work actually gets done

## Knowledge Distillation

Systematic conversion of expert judgment into machine-readable training signals.

**Example:** Healthcare RCM systems that:
- Identify gaps in coverage
- Formulate targeted questions
- Cross-check across multiple experts
- Synthesize into living knowledge base

## Learning Flywheel

From every decision: generate labeled examples (context + expert action)

- 50,000 cases/week × 3 decision points = 150,000 labeled examples/week

**Human-in-the-loop design:**
- Expert intervention at branch points
- Capture decision factors, not just outcomes

## Expertise Amplification

Goal: embed accumulated expertise of thousands of domain experts into AI platform

- Higher consistency
- Improved throughput
- Operators focus on consequential work
