# Cost per Token

**Category:** AI Economics / Metrics  
**Related:** [[AI Token Factories]], [[AI Infrastructure Investment]], AI TCO  

## Overview

Cost per Token is emerging as the primary metric for evaluating AI infrastructure economics in the generative and agentic AI era. This metric replaces traditional Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) calculations that were designed for data centers focused on storage and processing.

## Why It Matters

**The Shift:**
- Traditional data centers: Stored, retrieved, and processed data
- AI era facilities: Generate intelligence as tokens (AI token factories)
- Primary workload: Inference (not training)
- Primary output: Tokens

**The Problem with Traditional TCO:**
- Based on storage capacity, compute hours, data throughput
- Doesn't capture the economic reality of AI workloads
- Misaligns investment decisions with actual value creation

**The Solution:**
- Cost per token directly measures economic efficiency of AI infrastructure
- Aligns infrastructure investment with business outcomes
- Enables comparison across different infrastructure approaches

## Calculation Framework

```
Cost per Token = Total Infrastructure Cost / Total Tokens Generated
```

**Components:**
- Hardware costs (GPUs, networking, storage)
- Energy costs
- Operational costs
- Divided by token output (inference tokens)

## Strategic Implications

**For Infrastructure Planning:**
- Optimize for inference efficiency, not just training capability
- Evaluate vendors on token economics, not raw specs
- Consider total token throughput, not peak performance

**For Business Cases:**
- AI application economics depend on token costs
- Margin calculations require token cost understanding
- Pricing models should reflect token economics

**For Investment:**
- AI infrastructure ROI measured in token economics
- Capital allocation decisions based on cost-per-token projections
- Competitive advantage from lower cost-per-token

## Market Context

- **Q1 2026:** $300B venture quarter with massive AI infrastructure investment
- 80% of venture capital flowing to AI companies
- Inference buildout accelerating alongside training infrastructure

## Key Proponents

- **NVIDIA** - Advocating cost-per-token as primary metric
- AI infrastructure providers adopting token-based pricing

## Related Metrics

- Tokens per second (throughput)
- Tokens per watt (energy efficiency)
- Tokens per dollar (economic efficiency)

## Predictions

- Traditional TCO models will become obsolete for AI workloads
