# AI Token Factories

**Category:** AI Infrastructure  
**Related:** [[AI Infrastructure]], [[Cost per Token]], [[AI Inference]]  

## Overview

AI Token Factories is a concept describing the evolution of traditional data centers into facilities whose primary purpose is generating AI tokens (intelligence output) rather than storing or processing data. This represents a fundamental shift in how AI infrastructure is conceptualized and measured.

## Key Characteristics

- **Primary Output:** Intelligence manufactured in the form of tokens
- **Primary Workload:** AI inference (not training)
- **Economic Model:** Evaluated on cost-per-token rather than traditional TCO
- **Evolution:** Transformed from traditional data centers that stored/retrieved/processed data

## Economic Implications

**Traditional TCO Metrics (Obsolete):**
- Based on storage capacity
- Based on compute hours
- Based on data throughput

**New Metric:**
- **Cost per Token** - the only metric that matters for AI infrastructure economics

## Strategic Importance

The shift to AI token factories requires:
1. New economic models for infrastructure investment
2. Optimization for inference workloads over training
3. Rethinking total cost of ownership calculations
4. Focus on token output efficiency

## Market Context

- **Q1 2026:** $300B venture quarter with 80% flowing to AI companies
- Significant portion of investment going to AI infrastructure buildout
- Inference buildout often overlooked compared to training infrastructure

## Key Players

- **NVIDIA** - Leading proponent of AI token factory concept
- Cloud providers building AI-optimized infrastructure
- Enterprises building private AI factories
