# a16z $300B Quarter Analysis

## Key Data Points

- **Q1 2026:** $300B venture investment (record), up 150% YoY
- **AI share:** 80% of venture (up from 55% last year)
- **Mega-deals:** OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Waymo = 65% of total ($188B)
- **Excluding mega-deals:** $112B still = record most prior years

## Structural Insights

### 1. "Fewer bets, bigger checks"
- Seed deal count down 30% YoY
- Dollar investment up (seed +31%, early +41%)

### 2. "Bits to Atoms" Cycle
- Physical AI layer requiring factories, fabs, fleets
- Notable: Cerebras, Rapidus (chips), Skild AI (robots), Shield AI (defense)
- Unlike cloud/mobile (software-only)

### 3. Construction Productivity Stagnation
- $2T industry, no improvement in 75 years
- Goldman Sachs: regulation reduced growth by 0.7pp since 1960s
- Opportunity for robotics, prefab, AI construction

### 4. AI Inference Overtaking Training
- McKinsey: inference = 40%+ of data center demand by 2030 (35% CAGR)
- Training: 22% CAGR
- Global semiconductor market: $775B → $1.6T by 2030

### 5. Supply Chain Second-Order Effects
- Jet fuel +100% since Feb (Hormuz disruptions)
- Fertilizer costs rising (urea near 2022 highs)
- Nitrogen = 60% global fertilizer, 20% grain production costs
