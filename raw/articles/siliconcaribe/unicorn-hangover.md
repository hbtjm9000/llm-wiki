The Caribbean tech ecosystem should focus on sustainable, boring businesses rather than chasing unicorn dreams. The region needs more founders building practical solutions to local problems rather than chasing billion-dollar valuations that rarely materialize in island economies.

Key points:
- Unicorn culture distracts from practical, high-impact local solutions
- Caribbean founders can build for regional problems first
- Sustainable revenue beats hypothetical exits
