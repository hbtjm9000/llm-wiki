## Context & Why This Matters in 2026

A banking RFP is a formal request for selecting a banking provider, fintech vendor, or technology partner. In 2026, **stricter European regulation (DORA), rising cyber threats, and frameworks requiring auditability** mean institutions must prove a robust RFP process to board members, auditors, and regulators.

Use cases covered: transaction banking, cash management, core banking, custody, lending, onboarding KYC, and CRM.

> "Informal vendor selection creates avoidable risk. A repeatable banking RFP checklist helps compare responses, document decision making, protect reputation and move forward with confidence."

## The 10-Step Banking RFP Framework

### Step 1: Define Scope, Objectives & Success Metrics
Start with a clear problem statement, current state analysis, and business case *before* drafting any questions.

### Step 2: Assemble the RFP Team & Governance
- Must be **cross-functional** (prevents costly delays, conflicts, incomplete requirements)
- Establish an evaluation committee, conflict declarations, and board-level approval
- Final decision must be **traceable**

### Step 3: Build the Core Banking RFP Document
A comprehensive document generates comparable, complete responses. Core components:
- Introduction & overview
- Scope of work
- Required services
- Detailed instructions for proposals

**Typical length:** 5 to 40 pages.

### Step 4: Security, Data Sovereignty & Hosting
**Central to the checklist — not optional add-ons.**

Require vendors to answer:
> "State all non-negotiable security requirements, including SOC 2 Type II compliance and encryption standards such as TLS for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest. Require proof of rigorous security certifications from vendors to ensure compliance with security standards, including ISO 27001, penetration testing reports and audit history."

### Step 5: Process Workflow & Timelines
- Typical timeline: **10–12 weeks** from issuance to final selection for complex banking RFPs
- Use hard dates, single communication channel
- Document all vendor questions and publish official answers

### Step 6: Evaluation Criteria, Scoring Model & Final Selection

Publish weights to ensure a fair bidding process. Mandatory technical/legal compliance check first, then score using a weighted matrix:

| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---|
| Security, compliance and data sovereignty | **30%** |
| Technological capabilities and integration | 20% |
| Cost of services and pricing transparency | 20% |
| Quality of customer service and support | 10% |
| Implementation capability and expertise | 10% |
| Vendor stability, history and cultural fit | 10% |

- Use quantitative scoring + comments
- Narrow to 2–5 finalists
- Conduct interviews, check references, document alignment with long-term goals

### Step 7: Vendor Q&A, Clarifications & Communications
Organized communication protects fairness.

### Step 8: Implementation Planning & Post-Award Onboarding
> "The RFP process does not end at final selection."

Create a handover from the RFP team to operational owners.

### Step 9: Must-Have Sections in Any Banking RFP
**Common omissions to avoid:**
- Vague service levels (SLAs)
- Unclear pricing units
- Missing data residency demands
- Weak exit terms

### Step 10: Using Technology to Streamline RFPs
Use CRM and workflow platforms to create, distribute, and evaluate RFPs, automate manual effort, and maintain sovereignty.

## FAQ

**How often should a bank run an RFP for core services?**
Every 5–7 years, or earlier after regulatory change, merger, tech shift, or persistent service issues.

**RFI vs RFP?**
RFI explores the market (long list). RFP aligns with a defined scope and selects the provider.

---

## Key Takeaways for Banking RFP

1. Security/Compliance/Data Sovereignty is the single highest-weighted criterion (30%)
2. Cross-functional team is non-negotiable
3. 10-12 week typical process for complex banking RFPs
4. Post-award handoff must be planned from day one
5. Audit trail for every decision is a regulatory requirement, not a nice-to-have
